Pipeline Repair Economics

All pads share the line, so every pad shuts in for the repair and for any failure. The repair cost plus the production lost during the planned shutdown is weighed against the future production it preserves: current rates, refracs and development. The analysis runs at three levels: each pad alone, all pads combined, and doing nothing while carrying the risk of a failure. Each level gets a payback period and a break-even oil price over time.
